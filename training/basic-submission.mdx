## Who this training is for

Clinical site staff — for example radiotherapy technologists, medical physicists, or trial data managers — responsible for submitting patient imaging data to a clinical trial through Aitrium.

You will normally arrive at this page from a link in the Aitrium portal or AitriumOS, or from your trial coordinator, identifying it as the training for your trial. If you are unsure whether this is the right curriculum for your trial, ask your trial coordinator.

## What you will learn

- How to download and set up AitriumOS, including what your IT team needs to confirm before you start
- How the submission process protects patient privacy through validation, standardization, and de-identification
- How to submit a patient dataset, monitor its status, and resubmit when needed
- Where to get help if something goes wrong

## Curriculum

Review the following materials in order:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Getting Started">
    Read [Getting Started](/aitrium-os/overview), including the **Before You Begin** section — forward its IT requirements to your IT team if you have not already. Watch the installation and first-use videos on that page. *(~10 minutes)*
  </Step>
  <Step title="EORTC Data Submission">
    Read [EORTC Data Submission](/aitrium-os/no-protocol-submissions) and watch the step-by-step walkthrough video at the top of the page. Pay particular attention to the de-identification review step and the **Resubmitting a Dataset** section. *(~20 minutes)*
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **Critical behaviors.** Keep AitriumOS open until the **Submission complete** screen appears at the end of every upload, and always confirm the dataset shows as submitted on the trial page before you finish. Closing the application too early can leave a submission unregistered — the [EORTC Data Submission](/aitrium-os/no-protocol-submissions#5-upload-to-trial) guide covers both behaviors.
</Warning>

<Info>
  **Trial-specific instructions.** Some trials display additional instructions from the trial team in AitriumOS after you complete a submission. These supplement this training with requirements unique to your trial — review and follow them each time they appear.
</Info>

## Confirming your training

After reviewing the materials above, confirm completion in the Aitrium web portal. You must confirm before you can upload patient data to the trial.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your trials">
    Sign in to [Trial Participation](https://app.aitriumos.com/dashboard/participation) in the Aitrium portal. It lists the trials your site has been added to, and flags any trial waiting on your training. Select that trial. You can also click **Confirm training** on the **Confirm your training** card on your portal dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and confirm">
    In the **Complete the trial training** section, open the training curriculum if you haven't already, read the statement, tick the confirmation box, and click **Confirm training**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

One confirmation covers every trial that requires this training. Your confirmation — including the training version you reviewed and the date — is recorded for the trial organization's compliance records.

If the training materials are materially updated (for example, after a significant software change), you will be asked to review and confirm again before your next upload.

## Questions?

Contact your trial coordinator for trial-specific questions, or [support@aitriumos.com](mailto:support@aitriumos.com) for help with AitriumOS itself.
