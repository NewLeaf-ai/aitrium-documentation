## Who this training is for

Clinical site staff (for example radiotherapy technologists, medical physicists, or trial data managers) who submit patient imaging data to an EORTC trial through AitriumOS.

You will normally arrive at this page from a link in the Aitrium portal or AitriumOS, or from your trial coordinator, identifying it as the training for your trial. If you are unsure whether this is the right curriculum for your trial, ask your trial coordinator.

## What you will learn

- How to set up AitriumOS, including what your IT team needs to confirm before you start
- How to add a patient and check a dataset against the trial's dataset type
- How AitriumOS de-identifies DICOM metadata, and what you check yourself (images and documents)
- How to review and submit a dataset, check that it arrived, and send a new version when needed
- Where to get help if something goes wrong

## Curriculum

Review the following materials in order:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Getting Started">
    Read [Getting started](/aitrium-os/overview), including **Before You Begin**. Forward its IT requirements to your IT team if you haven't already. *(~10 minutes)*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Basic data submission guide and walkthrough">
    Read [EORTC data submission](/aitrium-os/no-protocol-submissions) and watch the walkthrough video at the top of the page. Pay particular attention to:
    - [Submission steps](/aitrium-os/no-protocol-submissions#submission-steps): check, standardize, de-identify, review, and upload
    - [Check the submission](/aitrium-os/no-protocol-submissions#check-the-submission) and [Send a new version](/aitrium-os/no-protocol-submissions#send-a-new-version)
    - [Supporting documents](/aitrium-os/documents-submissions#supporting-documents-in-an-imaging-submission), if your dataset types include documents
    - [Troubleshooting](/aitrium-os/submission-troubleshooting), for what AitriumOS does when something goes wrong

    *(~20 minutes)*
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  **Used AitriumOS 2.2 before?** Read [What's new in AitriumOS 2.3: If you submit to EORTC trials](/aitrium-os/whats-new-2-3#if-you-submit-to-eortc-trials).
</Tip>

<Warning>
  **Critical behaviors.** Keep AitriumOS open until **Submission complete** appears at the end of every upload, and always check that the dataset reads **Sent** on the trial page before you finish. See [Upload to trial](/aitrium-os/no-protocol-submissions#5-upload-to-trial) and [Check the submission](/aitrium-os/no-protocol-submissions#check-the-submission).
</Warning>

<Info>
  **Trial-specific instructions.** Some trials show instructions from the trial team on **Submission complete**. They add requirements unique to your trial. Follow them each time they appear.
</Info>

## Confirming your training

After reviewing the materials above, confirm completion in the Aitrium web portal. You must confirm before you can upload patient data to the trial.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your trials">
    Sign in to [Trial Participation](https://app.aitriumos.com/dashboard/participation) in the Aitrium portal. It lists the trials your site has been added to, and flags any trial waiting on your training. Select that trial. You can also click **Confirm training** on the **Confirm your training** card on your portal dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and confirm">
    In the **Complete the trial training** section, open the training curriculum if you haven't already, read the statement, select the confirmation checkbox, and click **Confirm training**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

One confirmation covers every trial that requires this training. Your confirmation — including the training version you reviewed and the date — is recorded for the trial organization's training records.

If the training materials are materially updated (for example, after a significant software change), you will be asked to review and confirm again before your next upload.
