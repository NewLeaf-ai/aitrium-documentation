## Overview

Aitrium's administration features give organization administrators centralized control over users, sites, integrations, and compliance. Admin features are available to users with the **Admin** role and are accessed from the **Admin** section in the Aitrium Portal sidebar.

Every organization on Aitrium has its own administrators, whether it runs trials (a **trial organization**) or contributes data from its sites (a **site organization**). Most admin pages apply to both; data transfer integrations are configured by the trial organization.

---

## Which pages apply to your organization

| Page | Trial organizations | Site organizations |
|------|---------------------|--------------------|
| [User Management](/aitrium-admin/user-management) | Yes | Yes |
| [Network Management](/aitrium-admin/network-management) | Yes — internal sites, plus external sites that Aitrium connects for multi-site trials | Yes — your internal sites |
| [Audit Logging](/aitrium-admin/audit-logging) | Yes | Yes |
| [Integrations](/aitrium-admin/integrations) | Yes — SFTP credentials and which sites and users may transfer data for each trial | No — transfer credentials are set by the trial organization. Upload destination results appear in your site's readiness ([details](/aitrium-admin/integrations#upload-destination-reachability)). |

## Roles & Permissions

Aitrium uses two customer-facing roles that determine what a user can access:

| Role | Scope | Admin Features |
|------|-------|----------------|
| **Admin** | Organization-wide | Full access to all admin features — user management, network, audit logs, and integrations. Not restricted to specific sites. |
| **User** | Site-specific | Standard access to workflows and data associated with their assigned site(s). No access to admin features. |

Admin users can manage all users and sites within their organization. User-role members only see data and workflows for the sites they've been assigned to.
